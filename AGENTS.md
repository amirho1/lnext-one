# Lnext — permanent project design principles

Read these instructions before implementing or modifying any UI. They apply to all current and future pages and components.

## Product and scope
- Lnext is a next-generation educational organization focused on education and career development.
- Communicate innovation, exploration, future possibilities, and institutional credibility.
- Preserve the business requirements, audience priorities, page structure, and functional limitations of the existing Lnext project brief. Do not invent requirements when that brief is unavailable; request it before establishing site structure or functionality.
- Balance creative expression with the professionalism expected by schools, parents, and institutional partners.
- Do not introduce unsupported functionality, including user accounts, login systems, or online payments.

## Visual direction
- Create a highly creative, futuristic, modern, visually distinctive experience inspired by sophisticated AI startups and innovative technology companies.
- Avoid generic educational templates and conventional corporate layouts.
- Build one coherent visual system across the entire site, including an impactful hero that immediately communicates Lnext's purpose.
- Use bold typography, purposeful asymmetry, creative spacing, layering, geometric compositions, and restrained interactive motion.
- Maintain credibility and readability; experimental design must serve communication and usability.

## Strict no-image policy
- Do not use images anywhere on the website, including heroes, sections, and secondary pages.
- No photographs, stock images, AI-generated images, image-based illustrations, or background images.
- Do not generate image assets or render existing placeholder imagery.
- Build visual interest exclusively from typography, color, CSS-generated geometry, patterns, grids, lines and visual effects, inline SVG geometric shapes, vector icons, and motion.
- Use CSS elements or inline SVG for geometry rather than image files or CSS background-image assets.

## Implementation standards
- Support Persian typography and right-to-left layouts; use semantic direction attributes and logical layout properties where appropriate.
- Maintain visual consistency and usability across mobile and desktop.
- Prioritize accessibility, semantic structure, keyboard navigation, visible focus, accessible contrast, and performance.
- Keep animation subtle and purposeful; respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Use interactions to clarify content rather than add decorative complexity.

## Before every UI change
1. Read these saved principles and the Lnext brief when available.
2. Follow the futuristic, creative visual direction.
3. Enforce the no-image policy across every page.
4. Compose visuals with shapes, typography, icons, and CSS/inline SVG effects.
5. Preserve the approved scope and desktop/mobile consistency.
